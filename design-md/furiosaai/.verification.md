## Capture history and scope limit

- **First run** (`--routes https://furiosa.ai/rngd,https://furiosa.ai/blog`, bundle kept at the session scratchpad as `furiosaai-run1-bundle.json`): 3 surfaces, coverage **54** (< 60), 5 component variants, 0 observed states — **preflight failed**.
- **Cause:** furiosa.ai opens two stacked corporate notice cards on every page (`div.cy-popup__panel`, 1440 × 900 at top 0): a recruitment-phishing warning ("[채용 사칭 주의] 개인정보 요구 피싱 사례 발생 안내") and a new-share issuance notice ("신주발행공고"). Their "Close" button carries neither a reject word nor a close `aria-label`, so the collector's reject-only dismissal step (Escape, reject selectors, `[aria-label*=close]`) left them open. With the cards over the viewport, the collector sampled only the header, the cards and a few content rows (39 / 37 elements on home and About) — no hero headline, product card, blog card or pointer state on furiosa.ai.
- **Retry with other surfaces** (the one permitted): home is always the first route; the other two became the Furiosa Access Program page (`lp.furiosa.ai`, HubSpot-hosted, no notice card) and About. Result: 3 surfaces, coverage **73**, 8 component variants, 3 observed state kinds, 1 interaction (form-error) — preflight passed. The rise comes from the landing page's form, not from better sampling of furiosa.ai; every token from `surface-2` is labelled as the Access Program page.
- No consent was granted and no dialog was accepted; the notice cards were never dismissed by hand.
