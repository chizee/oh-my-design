### Rendered official Kakao Login guide

The current official page states:

- Container: `#FEE500`
- Symbol: `#000000`
- Label: `#000000` at 85% opacity
- Colors outside the regulation must not be applied.
- Label font: OS default system typeface at 30Pt (Sp/Dp).
- Container radius: 12 pixels.
- The speech-bubble symbol's shape, proportion, and color cannot change; Kakao CI and functional icons cannot replace it; the button cannot omit it.
- Full labels: `카카오 로그인` / `Login with Kakao`; short labels: `로그인` / `Login`.
- When widening, preserve symbol and label zones and extend both sides equally. When scaling, preserve proportions and keep label height within one third of the container. Use the short label instead of distorting a smaller button.

### What was deliberately not declared

- `outline: auto` (rendered `#005fcc`) on all six controls is the browser's default ring, not Kakao's. No component carries it as a focus value; only the navigation pill's `#f3f3f3` fill is declared as focus.
- The navigation pill's fill persisting after blur is recorded as a lead (a script-applied current-item state is likely), not as a separate state.
- The search input lives in a layer opened by clicking the search button; the probe never clicks, so it is not measured. The July capture's `input.input_main` (fg `#ffffff`, 17px/700) belongs to that layer and was not promoted.
- The Developers documentation chrome (`surface-4`: sidebar buttons `#767676` → `#191919`, a 320px search button) is a separate evidence domain and was not added as Kakao components.
- The milestone combobox `pressed` (`#dfdfdf`) stays declared from its single frame. kakaocorp.com controls compute `transition: all 0s` wherever they were measured, so the frame is unlikely to be mid-transition, but no second sample exists.
- The theme and language toggles share the search button's style; they are folded into `search-control`, not added as components.
