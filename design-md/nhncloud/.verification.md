### Focus boundary

The collector hovers, presses and releases the mouse, then calls `.focus()`. A mouse press before `.focus()` leaves `:focus-visible` false (`docs/MEASUREMENT_METHOD_2026-09-22.md`), so no focus frame here is a keyboard focus measurement. No focus value is declared anywhere in this reference.
