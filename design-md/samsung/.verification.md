- Hover and pressed for the contained CTA, and hover for the underlined CTA: both were exercised with `:hover` / `:active` matched and nothing changed, so there is no value to declare.
- A pressed value for the AI-products and brand-identity search controls: their 2026-07-13 frames keep `#f7f7f7`, unlike the homepage probe. They are different pages, and no value was carried across.
- States for the brand-identity local navigation link: one element's `#555555` frames, with no sibling and no rest match, may be a transition frame.
- The carousel arrows: one is `disabled: true` in the capture; no component or state was written. Whether their `#007aff` is Swiper's default theme colour is a lead only.
- The footer droplist buttons (`button.droplist-button`, 11px/700, one variant `#2189ff`) carry `expanded` / `menu-open` names but no values, and their role is unconfirmed.
