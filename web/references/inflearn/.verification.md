### How the alpha steps were settled

The catalog chips (0.1 to 0.12), the hero icon buttons (0.2 to 0.4), and the GNB icon buttons (`#F8F9FA` to `rgba(241, 243, 245, 0.65)`) change only a fill's alpha or move to a translucent value. An alpha step always lies on the line from the rest fill toward its opaque colour, so that test alone cannot separate a design step from the start of a transition. What separates them here is timing: the collector's frames drift (the search input's border reads differently on each route), while every chip, icon button, and GNB button records one exact value in both frames. They are declared as settled.
